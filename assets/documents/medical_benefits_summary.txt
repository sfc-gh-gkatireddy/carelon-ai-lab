Blue Advantage Medical Benefits Summary
Elevance Health — Plan Year 2026
Document ID: MED-2026-002
Effective: January 1, 2026

SECTION 1 — PREVENTIVE CARE

Preventive services aligned with USPSTF A and B recommendations are covered
at 100% with no member cost sharing when rendered by an in-network provider:

- Annual wellness visit (CPT 99391, 99395): one per plan year
- Colorectal cancer screening (CPT 45378 colonoscopy): members age 45-75,
  once every 10 years, or fecal immunochemical test annually
- Mammography: members age 40+, annually
- Diabetes screening (HbA1c, CPT 83036): members with risk factors, annually
- Immunizations (CPT Z23-related administration codes): no cost share
- Annual lipid panel and blood pressure screening

Diagnostic (as opposed to screening) colonoscopies are subject to normal
cost sharing: 20% coinsurance after deductible for PPO plans, $25 copayment
for HMO plans.

SECTION 2 — OFFICE VISITS AND SPECIALIST REFERRALS

Primary care office visit (CPT 99213-99215):
- HMO/EPO: $25 copayment
- PPO: $30 copayment in-network, 40% coinsurance out-of-network
- HDHP: deductible applies, then 20% coinsurance

Specialist visits:
- HMO plans require a referral from the assigned PCP before a specialist
  visit. Services rendered without a referral are not covered except for
  OB/GYN direct access and behavioral health.
- PPO plans do not require referrals but out-of-network cost sharing applies
  when the provider is out-of-network.

SECTION 3 — EMERGENCY CARE

Emergency room visits (CPT 99284, 99285) are covered at all facilities
regardless of network status. Cost sharing is applied at the in-network
level when the member presents with a prudent layperson emergency:
- HMO/EPO: $150 copayment, waived if admitted
- PPO: $150 copayment, waived if admitted
- Ground ambulance: $100 copayment

Post-stabilization services and observation follow in-network rules; members
should transfer to an in-network facility when medically appropriate.

SECTION 4 — MENTAL HEALTH AND BEHAVIORAL HEALTH

Mental health parity applies. In-person psychotherapy (CPT 90834, 90837):
- HMO: $25 copayment, no PCP referral required for behavioral health
- PPO: $30 copayment in-network
Telebehavioral health visits are covered at the same cost sharing level.
Annual maximum: unlimited visits subject to medical necessity review after
20 visits per year.

SECTION 5 — PHYSICAL THERAPY

Physical therapy (CPT 97110, 97140):
- Covered when prescribed for restoration of function following injury,
  surgery, or acute exacerbation of a chronic condition
- HMO: $25 copayment per visit; PPO: $30 copayment
- Prior authorization required after 12 visits per condition per plan year
- Maintenance therapy for chronic conditions without functional improvement
  documentation is not covered

SECTION 6 — DURABLE MEDICAL EQUIPMENT (DME)

- Covered at 20% coinsurance after deductible (all plan types)
- Requires prior authorization for items over $500 (wheelchairs, hospital
  beds, CPAP machines)
- CPAP machines require documented sleep study (AHI >= 5 with symptoms)
- Rental vs purchase: equipment rented for 10 months is considered purchased

SECTION 7 — SURGICAL SERVICES

Inpatient surgery (CPT 27447 total knee replacement, 47562 cholecystectomy,
29881 knee arthroscopy, 45380 colonoscopy with biopsy):
- Prior authorization required for all inpatient surgical admissions and
  elective outpatient surgical procedures
- HMO: 10% coinsurance; PPO: 10% in-network, 40% out-of-network
- Centers of Excellence program: knee and hip replacement at designated
  facilities have $0 member cost share

SECTION 8 — ADVANCED IMAGING

CT (CPT 70450, 74177) and echocardiography (93351):
- Prior authorization required for non-emergency advanced imaging
- HMO: $100 copayment; PPO: $100 in-network, 50% out-of-network
- Chest X-ray (71046): no PA required, $25 copayment

SECTION 10 — CLAIMS AND APPEALS

Claims must be submitted within 12 months of the date of service. Appeals of
denied claims follow the same three-level process as pharmacy appeals:
internal Level 1 (30 days), internal Level 2 with medical director review
(30 days), then external independent review (4 months to request).
