Blue Advantage Quality and Stars Measures Reference
Elevance Health — Plan Year 2026
Document ID: QUAL-2026-003
Effective: January 1, 2026

This document summarizes the HEDIS measures and CMS Star Ratings criteria
tracked for the commercial and Medicare Advantage lines of business.

SECTION 1 — COLORECTAL CANCER SCREENING (HEDIS COL)

Measure definition: Members age 45-75 who had appropriate screening for
colorectal cancer during the measurement year or the two years prior.
Appropriate screenings are:
- Fecal immunochemical test (FIT) annually
- Colonoscopy (CPT 45378) during the measurement year or prior two years
- FIT-DNA test within the prior 3 years

Eligible population: Members enrolled as of December 1 of the measurement
year with at least 11 months of continuous enrollment. Members with a
history of colorectal cancer or total colectomy are excluded.

Compliance target: 80% of eligible members.

SECTION 2 — DIABETES CARE MEASURES

2.1 HbA1c Testing (HEDIS CDC-A1c)
Members age 18-75 with Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes (ICD-10 E10.x, E11.x) who
had an HbA1c test (CPT 83036) during the measurement year.
Target: 92% of eligible members.

2.2 HbA1c Poor Control (HEDIS CDC-A1cP)
Percentage of diabetic members whose most recent HbA1c is greater than 9.0%.
Lower is better. Target: fewer than 15% of eligible members.

2.3 Eye Exam (HEDIS CDC-Eye)
Members with diabetes who had a retinal or dilated eye exam by an
ophthalmologist or optometrist, or documented retinal imaging.
Target: 70% of eligible members.

2.4 Kidney Health Monitoring (HEDIS CDC-Kidney)
Members with diabetes who had an eGFR or urine albumin test during the
year. Target: 55% of eligible members.

SECTION 3 — MEDICATION ADHERENCE MEASURES

3.1 Diabetes Medication Adherence (PDC-DR)
Percentage of diabetic members age 18+ with at least two fills of a diabetes
medication (Biguanide, Sulfonylurea, GLP-1, Insulin, SGLT2, DPP-4 classes)
whose proportion of days covered (PDC) is at least 80% during the measurement
year. Target: 85% of eligible members.

Adherence calculation: PDC = total days supply across all fills / days in
measurement period. Members with days supply exceeding 365 count as 365.

3.2 Statin Adherence for Members with Cardiovascular Disease (PDC-STA)
Members with atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ICD-10 I20.x, I25.x)
with two or more statin fills and PDC >= 80%. Target: 85%.

3.3 RAS Antagonist Adherence (PDC-RASA)
Members with hypertension (ICD-10 I10.x) with two or more fills of an ACE
inhibitor or ARB and PDC >= 80%. Target: 85%.

SECTION 4 — PREVENTIVE SCREENING MEASURES

4.1 Breast Cancer Screening (HEDIS BCS)
Members age 50-74 with a mammogram during the measurement year or the two
years prior. Target: 70%.

4.2 Adult BMI Assessment
Adult members with a documented BMI during the measurement year or the two
years prior. Target: 90%.

4.3 Controlling High Blood Pressure (HEDIS CBP)
Members age 18-85 with hypertension whose most recent systolic reading is
< 140 mmHg and diastolic is < 90 mmHg. Target: 75%.

SECTION 5 — CARE GAP IDENTIFICATION METHODOLOGY

A care gap is a member who is eligible for a measure but shows no compliant
claim activity in the claims data:
- Colorectal screening gap: age 45-75, no colonoscopy claim (CPT 45378) and
  no FIT claim in the prior 3 years
- A1c testing gap: diabetes diagnosis, no 83036 lab claim in the current year
- Adherence gap: PDC below 80% based on pharmacy fill dates and days supply
- Statin gap for CAD members: no statin fill in the last 6 months

Gap lists are refreshed monthly and prioritized by member risk tier. Care
managers should prioritize members with both chronic conditions and multiple
concurrent gaps.

SECTION 6 — STAR RATINGS WEIGHTING (MEDICARE ADVANTAGE)

Colorectal cancer screening, diabetes A1c testing, and medication adherence
measures are triple-weighted or double-weighted domains in the CMS Star
Ratings for Medicare Advantage plans. Improvements in these measures have
the largest impact on overall Star rating.

For data questions, contact the Quality Analytics team. Measure
specifications follow NCQA HEDIS Volume 2 definitions for the measurement
year.
