Blue Advantage Formulary Guidelines
Elevance Health — Plan Year 2026
Document ID: FORM-2026-001
Effective: January 1, 2026

SECTION 1 — FORMULARY TIERS

All covered outpatient drugs are assigned to one of four formulary tiers.
Tier placement determines member cost sharing:

Tier 1 — Preferred Generic: $10 copayment (30-day supply)
Tier 2 — Preferred Brand: $45 copayment (30-day supply)
Tier 3 — Non-Preferred Brand: $85 copayment (30-day supply)
Tier 4 — Specialty: 25% coinsurance, $150 maximum per fill

Examples by tier:
Tier 1: Metformin, Lisinopril, Atorvastatin, Sertraline, Amlodipine, Furosemide, Prednisone
Tier 2: Rosuvastatin, Escitalopram, Fluticasone/Salmeterol, Empagliflozin
Tier 3: Liraglutide, Sitagliptin, Apixaban, Tiotropium
Tier 4: Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Insulin Glargine (brand), Insulin Lispro (brand)

SECTION 2 — PRIOR AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENTS

The following drug classes require prior authorization (PA) before dispensing.
PA requests are reviewed within 72 hours for standard requests and 24 hours
for urgent requests. A completed PA is valid for 12 months from approval date.

2.1 GLP-1 Agonists (Semaglutide, Liraglutide, Tirzepatide)
GLP-1 medications require PA for all indications. Approval criteria:
- Diagnosis of Type 2 diabetes mellitus (ICD-10 E11.x) with HbA1c >= 7.5% on two
  consecutive readings within the past 6 months, OR
- BMI >= 35 with at least one obesity-related comorbidity (hypertension,
  hyperlipidemia, or obstructive sleep apnea)
- Documented trial of at least two formulary alternatives (Metformin plus one
  other oral antidiabetic) for at least 90 days at therapeutic dose, unless
  contraindicated
- For weight management indications: BMI >= 30 (or >= 27 with comorbidity) and
  documented participation in a structured lifestyle modification program for
  at least 6 months
Note: GLP-1 agents for cosmetic or lifestyle use without qualifying diagnosis
are not covered under any circumstance. Step therapy requirements are not
waived for member convenience.

2.2 Insulin Products (Brand)
Brand-name insulins (Insulin Glargine, Insulin Lispro, Humalog Mix 75/25)
require PA only when a biosimilar or preferred basal insulin is available
on formulary and the member has no documented adverse reaction to the
preferred product. Humalog Mix 75/25 requires step therapy through basal-bolus
regimen evaluation.

2.3 SGLT2 Inhibitors (Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin)
PA required when prescribed for indications other than Type 2 diabetes or
heart failure. For heart failure indication (I50.x), PA is auto-approved with
documented diagnosis.

2.4 Anticoagulants (Apixaban)
PA required for members newly starting therapy. Continuation of existing
therapy is auto-approved with pharmacy claims history demonstrating at
least 6 months of continuous use.

2.5 Specialty Tier Drugs
All Tier 4 drugs require PA and enrollment in the Blue Specialty Pharmacy
program. Home delivery is available for maintenance fills.

SECTION 3 — STEP THERAPY REQUIREMENTS

Step therapy requires members to try preferred alternatives before
non-preferred drugs are approved:

Step 1 (Diabetes): Metformin or Metformin ER for 90 days
Step 2 (Diabetes): Add a sulfonylurea, DPP-4 inhibitor, or SGLT2 inhibitor
Step 3 (Diabetes): GLP-1 agonist or insulin therapy may be considered

Failure on Step 1 or Step 2 is defined as: HbA1c reduction of less than
0.5 percentage points after 90 days at maximally tolerated dose, or
documented intolerance (GI side effects, lactic acidosis risk, hypoglycemia
events requiring medical intervention).

SECTION 4 — QUANTITY LIMITS

- GLP-1 agonists: 1 pen per 28 days (max 30-day supply per fill)
- Insulins: 2 vials or 2 boxes of pens per 28 days
- All maintenance medications: up to 90-day supply permitted at retail
  after second fill

SECTION 5 — APPEALS PROCESS

Members or prescribers may appeal a denied prior authorization:
1. Level 1 appeal: Submit within 60 calendar days of the denial letter date.
   Include clinical documentation supporting medical necessity. Standard
   appeal decision issued within 30 calendar days; urgent appeals within
   72 hours.
2. Level 2 appeal: If Level 1 is upheld, members may request an independent
   external review by the state Department of Insurance within 4 months of
   the Level 1 decision.
3. Peer-to-peer review: Prescribers may request a direct discussion with
   the medical director before formal appeal submission. This does not
   extend appeal deadlines.

SECTION 6 — EXCLUSIONS

- Over-the-counter medications unless specifically listed as covered
- Fertility medications under non-infertility diagnoses
- Weight loss supplements and over-the-counter weight management products
- Compounded medications without FDA-approved equivalents
- Drugs prescribed for off-label use without supporting PA documentation

For questions, contact the Pharmacy Benefit Management unit or call the
number on the member ID card.
